PhD Researcher • Agentic AI for HPC

Prachi Jadhav

University of Tennessee, Knoxville  ·  Oak Ridge National Laboratory

Open to research collaborations

I’m a PhD researcher working on AI-driven resource management for supercomputing systems. My work focuses on designing intelligent, multi-agent methods that can coordinate, adapt to dynamic workloads, and make better decisions than traditional rule-based schedulers.

I build simulation environments and testbeds to evaluate how these systems improve efficiency, fairness, and reliability in large-scale computing—laying the groundwork for more autonomous and scalable supercomputers.

At Stacklok, I worked as an ML Research Software Engineer, focusing on the AI for security domain, building models for classification and anomaly detection in software and system-level data. I focused on designing scalable pipelines and improving detection accuracy for real-world security use cases.

Previously, I completed my MS in Computer Science at the University of Memphis. In my master’s thesis under Dr. Deepak Venugopal, I studied bias in deep generative models such as Variational Autoencoders and PixelCNN++, and its impact on downstream tasks like classification. I developed an importance sampling approach to reduce bias in generated data and improve sample quality. Building on this, I designed a semi-supervised learning pipeline using semantic loss to enhance classification performance with limited labeled data.
